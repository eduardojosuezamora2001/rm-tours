# RM Tours

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Users
Spanish- and English-speaking travelers exploring tours in Paquera, Tambor, Montezuma and Santa Teresa. The owner maintains the catalogue; a coordinator confirms requests through WhatsApp.

## Product Purpose
Help visitors choose a tour and plan, understand its information and optional USD price, and prepare a complete WhatsApp quotation request with few steps.

## Capabilities and Constraints
React, shadcn, React Router, nuqs, Hono and Supabase; independently deployable frontend and API on Cloudflare Workers. Spanish and English public interface; Spanish administration at /admin-tours. One owner account, no public registration. No online payments, bookings, inventory or automatic WhatsApp delivery. Quote personal information stays in memory.

## Brand Commitments
Use the existing RM Tours Stitch project 4366171717337285856 as visual authority. Ocean blue, forest green, coastal photography, Montserrat headings and Plus Jakarta Sans body text.

## Evidence on Hand
Stitch desktop and mobile home, catalogue, detail, administration designs and the attached requirements CSV were read through its MCP. Claims, sample reviews, sample contact details and sample prices in design mockups are not verified production content.

## Product Principles
- Prefer a clear path from discovery to WhatsApp.
- Publish only owner-approved, bilingual content.
- Protect drafts and owner capabilities at both API and database boundaries.
- Show illustrative content only in an explicitly enabled development demo.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Responsive web; keyboard access, semantic controls, visible focus, readable contrast and reduced-motion support.
